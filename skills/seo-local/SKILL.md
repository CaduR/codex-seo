# Local SEO review

Confirm the business name, service area, and locations from supplied evidence. Review consistency of name, address, and phone where published; location-page usefulness; local intent and service details; review response practices; and relevant LocalBusiness markup. Treat Google Business Profile details as unverified unless supplied or directly accessible.

Prioritize accuracy and useful local landing pages. Do not recommend fake locations, fabricated reviews, doorway pages, or keyword-stuffed business names. Paid map-grid or citation data is not required; disclose when such data is unavailable.
