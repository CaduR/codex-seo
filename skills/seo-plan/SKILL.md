# SEO strategy and roadmap

Ground the plan in the business model, target audience, geography, products/services, current site structure, constraints, and success measures. Ask for missing inputs only when they would change the recommendation; otherwise label assumptions.

Build a phased roadmap that connects technical prerequisites, important page improvements, content opportunities, internal linking, and measurement. Rank tasks by expected user/business value, confidence in the evidence, effort, dependencies, and risk. Treat competitor and keyword metrics as dated provider observations, not facts about future demand. Distinguish strategic hypotheses from verified site issues.

For each initiative provide the reason, target pages or audience, owner/input needed, dependency, rough effort band, and a measurable indicator where one can reasonably be observed. Avoid guaranteeing traffic, ranking, or revenue and do not invent forecasts. End with a short first-phase action list and the data that would improve the next planning pass.

This skill does not include automated market research, keyword-volume datasets, or project management integration. Paid data providers are optional and require a cost estimate and user approval before use.
