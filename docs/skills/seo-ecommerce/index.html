E-commerce SEO

When local tools are ready, use seo_analyze with render_page.py for accessible product pages and pass user-supplied Product JSON-LD through input_text to schema_ecommerce_validate.py. The default MCP has no inventory, pricing feed, Merchant Center, Amazon, or marketplace connection.

Map the store's product and category page types, faceted navigation, canonical/indexation policy, product availability and pricing, internal links, and page-specific copy. Review structured data against visible and current product facts. Do not generate price, stock, review, GTIN, shipping, or return-policy claims without verified data.

Check that category pages help shoppers choose, product pages answer decision questions, variants are represented consistently, and filtered URLs do not create uncontrolled duplication. Treat feeds and marketplace listings as distinct channels with their own current requirements. When comparing products or prices, cite dated sources and state coverage.

Return findings by page template, the evidence, risk, and practical fix. No review markup should imply ratings not visible and valid schema does not guarantee rich results. Merchant Center, Shopping, Amazon, and competitor data require their own access; this skill does not connect to those accounts or crawl a catalog automatically. Avoid bulk changes without explicit approval.

O que esperar

As recomendações devem ser ligadas a evidências observadas. Se uma página, conta ou fonte de dados não estiver acessível, o resultado precisa indicar essa limitação.

Dados do Google, backlinks, posições e buscas atuais não são presumidos. Conexões de terceiros podem exigir conta, permissão e custos próprios.
